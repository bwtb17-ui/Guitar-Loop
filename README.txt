Guitar Loop App

How to use:
1. Host index.html on HTTPS (GitHub Pages works well).
2. Open it in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Allow microphone access.
4. Tap Record and play your rhythm.
5. Tap Stop.
6. Tap Loop to hear it repeat.
7. Drag the orange start/end markers or use the sliders and 10 ms nudge buttons.
8. Save the loop to your browser, or export it as a WAV file.

Notes:
- Saved loops use IndexedDB and remain in that browser unless site data is cleared.
- WAV export makes a separate file you can back up or share.
- Headphones are recommended so loop playback does not feed back into the microphone.
- Microphone recording requires HTTPS (or localhost).
